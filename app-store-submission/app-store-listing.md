## Description (4000 char max)

Sparkling Silver is the official wholesale ordering app for verified stockists and retail partners of Sparkling Silver. Browse our complete 925 sterling silver catalogue, place orders with exact gross weights, add per-item remarks, and track every SKU from confirmation to dispatch — all from your phone.

Access is limited to approved wholesale buyers. Accounts are created by our team, so what you see is your pricing, your order history and your stock — nothing else.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Browse the full catalogue
Explore two complete collections — 925 Ultra Antique Jewellery and 925 Premium CZ Jewellery — across necklaces, long sets, chokers, jhumkas, tops, bangles, bracelets, bajus, belts, matil, tikka, mangal sutra, brooches and more. Every piece is photographed on our studio jewellery stands so you can judge drape, length and finish before you order.

See real product detail
Full-screen studio imagery, complete SKU codes, gross weight per piece, minimum order quantity and subcategory details on every product page. Zoom in on the workmanship instead of guessing from a catalogue sheet.

Order with a proper cart
Add items, set quantities in bulk, and attach a written remark to any individual SKU — plating notes, packing instructions, size preferences, anything your order needs. Your cart carries the running total gross weight so you always know the size of the parcel you are booking.

Edit before you commit
Change quantities or remove a SKU from an order after placing it, right up until it moves into production. Cancel individual items instead of scrapping the whole order.

Track every order, item by item
Live status for each SKU — pending, accepted, confirmed, processing, dispatched. Partial shipments are shown separately, so a split order never leaves you guessing which pieces have already left our workshop.

Wishlist and saved details
Save designs you want to revisit, keep your shipping addresses on file, and reorder past sets in a couple of taps.

Stay updated
Opt in to push notifications for order status changes, dispatch confirmations and new collection drops.

BUILT FOR THE TRADE
- Gross weight shown at item, SKU and order level
- Minimum order quantity enforced per design
- Per-item remarks carried straight through to our production floor
- Full order history with itemised weights
- Direct WhatsApp line to our sales desk

ABOUT SPARKLING SILVER
We manufacture 925 sterling silver jewellery for the Indian wholesale market — antique-finish bridal pieces and premium CZ designs made for weddings, festivals and daily wear. Every design in this app comes from our own workshop.

Not yet a partner? Reach us through the Contact screen or on WhatsApp and our team will set up your account.

Note: This app requires an account issued by Sparkling Silver. There is no public sign-up.
